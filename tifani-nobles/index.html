About Tifani

Support rooted in empathy and real-world understanding.

With over 15 years in the social service field, Tifani has supported individuals and families as they work through anxiety, stress, grief, and family restructuring while pursuing healthier and more meaningful lives.

She holds a Bachelor's degree in Human Services with a discipline in Psychology from the University of South Alabama and a Master's degree in Marriage and Family Counseling from the University of Mobile. She is working toward full licensure as an Associate Licensed Counselor in Alabama under the supervision of Cindy Perronne, MS, ADC, LPS-S.

What therapy is like with Tifani

Tifani's approach is warm, collaborative, and supportive. She draws from Family Systems, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Motivational Interviewing, and Solution-Focused strategies, and also provides EMDR clinical sessions upon request.
