About Tifani

Support rooted in empathy and real-world understanding.

It takes courage to reach out for support, especially during times when life feels vulnerable, overwhelming, or uncertain. Tifani supports clients who are navigating complex life changes, heavy emotions, or feeling disconnected from themselves, helping them feel more grounded, connected, and aligned with the person they aspire to be.

With over 15 years in the social service field and experience working closely with youth, adults, and families, Tifani understands the complexities of life. She welcomes individuals and families from all walks of life and strives to offer a space where clients can feel safe, accepted, and free to be themselves without judgment.

She holds a Bachelor's degree in Human Services with a discipline in Psychology from the University of South Alabama and a Master's degree in Marriage and Family Counseling from the University of Mobile. She is working toward full licensure as an Associate Licensed Counselor in Alabama under the supervision of Cindy Perronne, MS, ADC, LPS-S.

What therapy is like with Tifani

Tifani's approach is warm, collaborative, and supportive. She tailors sessions to each client's needs and goals, working together toward lasting, meaningful changes that can lead to a more purposeful and fulfilling life. She draws from Family Systems, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Motivational Interviewing, and Solution-Focused strategies, and also provides EMDR clinical sessions upon request.
