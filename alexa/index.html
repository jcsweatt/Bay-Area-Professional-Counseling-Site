About Alexa

Care grounded in understanding and practical support.

Alexa moved from San Diego to Mobile to pursue a Bachelor of Science degree in Psychology while playing collegiate softball at the University of Mobile. She later earned her Master of Arts degree in Psychology while serving as a full-time graduate assistant for the athletic department, completing the program at the top of her class.

Her experience includes six years working with individuals experiencing opiate and other substance addictions, leadership in a transitions program for high-risk youth in Baldwin County, and five years providing outpatient drug and alcohol and family therapy in Mobile Public Schools.

What therapy is like with Alexa

Alexa understands that no two individuals arrive in therapy with the same concerns or goals. Her approach is customized to meet each client where they are, combining warmth and collaboration with practical therapeutic strategies.

She frequently draws from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Motivational Interviewing, Solution-Focused Therapy, and Existential Therapy.
