About Leigh

Support through vulnerable and meaningful seasons.

Leigh is an Associate Licensed Counselor who provides compassionate, evidence-based support to individuals navigating perinatal mental health, anxiety, depression, grief, and trauma. She is under the supervision of Lacey Moore, MS, LPC-S, MAC.

She specializes in perinatal mental health, supporting clients through pregnancy, postpartum, infertility, and pregnancy loss, and has received advanced training through Postpartum Support International. She also has extensive experience working with trauma across the lifespan, including children as young as four.

What therapy is like with Leigh

Leigh fosters a therapeutic environment grounded in empathy, trust, and meaningful connection. She incorporates Brainspotting, Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Adlerian Play Therapy, and developmentally appropriate play-based approaches.
