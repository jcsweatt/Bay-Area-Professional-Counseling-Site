Therapy language, made simpler

Therapy Approaches, Explained

Different approaches, one goal: helping you feel better, understand yourself more clearly, and move toward a life that feels meaningful.

CBT

CBT

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

Helps you identify and change unhelpful thought patterns and behaviors so you can feel better and cope more effectively.

ACT

ACT

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy

Helps you accept difficult thoughts and feelings while taking steps toward a life that feels meaningful to you.

PC

Person-Centered Therapy

A supportive, nonjudgmental approach that puts you in the driver's seat while we focus on your growth and well-being.

SFBT

SFBT

Solution-Focused Brief Therapy

Focuses on your strengths and what's already working to help you find solutions and move toward your goals.

DBT

DBT

Dialectical Behavior Therapy

Combines acceptance and change strategies to help you manage intense emotions, build skills, and improve relationships.

MB

Mindfulness-Based Therapy

Uses mindfulness practices to help you stay present, reduce stress, and build greater awareness and compassion.

TI

Trauma-Informed Care

An approach that recognizes how trauma can affect your life and prioritizes safety, trust, choice, and empowerment.

AB

Attachment-Based Therapy

Explores how early relationships shape the way you connect with yourself and others, and how to build healthier bonds.

IFS

IFS

Internal Family Systems

Helps you understand different parts of yourself with compassion so you can heal and feel more balanced inside.

EMDR

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitization & Reprocessing

A structured approach that helps the brain process and heal from distressing experiences.

ST

Somatic Therapy

Focuses on the connection between your body and mind to help release stress, trauma, and emotional tension.

ET

Existential Therapy

Explores life's bigger questions, like purpose, freedom, and meaning, to help you live with greater authenticity.

FS

Family Systems

Looks at how family patterns, roles, and relationships shape individual well-being and support healthier ways of relating.

CT

Couples Therapy

Supports partners in building understanding, improving communication, and creating a stronger, healthier relationship.

SB

Strengths-Based Therapy

Focuses on your unique strengths and resources to help you overcome challenges and thrive.

NT

Narrative Therapy

Looks at the stories you tell about your life and helps you rewrite them in ways that support healing and growth.

MI

Motivational Interviewing

A collaborative approach that helps you explore ambivalence, clarify values, and strengthen motivation for change.

PP

Positive Psychology

Builds on strengths, meaning, gratitude, resilience, and well-being alongside work on problems or symptoms.

TF

TF-CBT

Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

Uses trauma-sensitive CBT tools to help children, teens, and families process traumatic experiences safely.

BS

Brainspotting

Uses eye position and focused attention to help access and process distress, trauma, and emotional blocks.

PT

Play Therapy

Uses play, creativity, and developmentally appropriate tools to help children express feelings and build coping skills.

GT

Gestalt Therapy

Focuses on present-moment awareness, personal responsibility, and integrating thoughts, feelings, and actions.

BM

Behavior Modification

Uses practical strategies and reinforcement to build helpful behaviors and reduce patterns that are getting in the way.

FB

Faith-Based Counseling

Integrates a client's faith, values, and spiritual resources into therapy when that support feels important to them.

Therapy is not one-size-fits-all. Your therapist will work with you to find the approach, or blend of approaches, that fits you best.
