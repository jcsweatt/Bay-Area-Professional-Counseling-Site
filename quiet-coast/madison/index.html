About Madison

Compassionate support across ages and needs.

Madison is passionate about supporting clients of all ages, including adults and children on the autism spectrum, as they work through anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, and other life challenges. She hopes to be a helping hand and steady shoulder clients can rely on as they move through difficult seasons.

She earned her Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from Huntingdon College and completed her Master's in Clinical Mental Health Counseling at the University of West Alabama. Her professional experience includes working as a Registered Behavior Technician with children of all ages on the autism spectrum, as well as internship experience at AltaPointe.

What therapy is like with Madison

Madison is an Associate Licensed Counselor under the supervision of Lacey Moore, LPC-S. She draws from CBT, DBT, faith-based counseling, behavior modification, and Gestalt therapy, shaping care around each client's age, needs, and goals.
