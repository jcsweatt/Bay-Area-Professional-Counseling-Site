About Lanya

A relationship where you can let your guard down.

Lanya believes the therapy process involves forming a relationship with someone clients can trust with their innermost thoughts and struggles. She provides an environment free from judgment where clients can let their guard down, be themselves, and understand that asking for help is not a sign that something is wrong, but a sign of readiness to grow.

What therapy is like with Lanya

With more than five years assisting individuals through substance use, anxiety, depression, self-esteem concerns, anger management, and other life issues, Lanya's client-centered approach begins with the belief that no one knows you better than you. She offers encouragement, guidance, perspective, and support as clients discover answers within themselves and make changes that can improve many parts of life.
