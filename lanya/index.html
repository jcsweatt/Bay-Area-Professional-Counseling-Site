About Lanya

A relationship where you can let your guard down.

Lanya believes the therapy process involves forming a relationship with someone with whom clients can share their thoughts and struggles. She provides an environment free from judgment, where seeking help is understood as readiness to grow and better understand yourself.

What therapy is like with Lanya

With more than ten years supporting individuals through substance use challenges, anxiety, depression, self-esteem concerns, anger management, and major life transitions, Lanya's client-centered approach begins with the belief that no one knows you better than you. She offers encouragement, perspective, and steady support as clients uncover their strengths and create meaningful change.
