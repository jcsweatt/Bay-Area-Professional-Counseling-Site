About Deena

A space to feel understood and seen.

Life can be overwhelming while still offering moments of growth and beauty. Deena supports clients who may be managing ADHD, the emotional weight of anxiety, or questions about what comes next in life.

As a multicultural female counselor, Deena values being understood and heard, creating a space where similarities and differences are respected and celebrated. She is an Associate Licensed Counselor in Alabama under the supervision of Cindy Perronne, MS, ADC, LPS-S.

What therapy is like with Deena

Deena believes therapy does not need to feel intimidating. She offers a safe, judgment-free environment that moves at each client's pace, using CBT, ACT, person-centered, and Solution-Focused Brief Therapy approaches to explore experiences and identify strengths.
