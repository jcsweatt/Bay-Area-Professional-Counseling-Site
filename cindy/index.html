About Cindy

Experienced support for growth and self-awareness.

Cindy is a Licensed Professional Counselor and Certified Alcohol and Drug Counselor with over 20 years of experience in the counseling profession.

She earned her Bachelor of Science in Psychology from the University of Southern Mississippi in 1999 and her Master of Science in Community Counseling from the University of South Alabama in 2002. She is a member of the Alabama Counseling Association and the American Society for Reproductive Medicine.

What therapy is like with Cindy

Cindy works to build trust with clients while providing psychoeducation and supporting self-awareness and change. Her therapeutic approaches include person-centered therapy, positive psychology, Motivational Interviewing, and Cognitive Behavioral Therapy.

Who Cindy works with

  • Adults
  • Couples
  • Adolescents
  • Mental health concerns
