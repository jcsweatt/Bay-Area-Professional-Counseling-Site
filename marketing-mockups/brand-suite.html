Marketing Suite Concept

A more elevated collateral direction for Bay Area Professional Counseling: rounded corners, tactile paper tones, quiet succulents, warm mossy greens, and enough structure to feel professional without becoming sterile.

The Story

A practice identity that feels grounded before a client ever calls.

This suite is built around the idea that reaching out for therapy is both practical and emotional. The materials should feel organized enough to build trust, but soft enough to lower the temperature in the room. The design borrows from the website's Gulf Coast porch, quiet succulents, moss, paper texture, and rounded containers so every touchpoint feels like part of the same calm practice experience.

Rounded corners

Chosen because they echo the website containers and feel approachable in hand. No sharp square cards, no clinical edge.

Transparent logo treatment

The logo sits directly on paper, color, or imagery. It is not boxed in, because the transparency should feel lighter and more integrated.

Botanical texture

Succulents and grasses are intentionally low contrast, used as atmosphere rather than decoration that competes with names, credentials, or contact details.

Color System

Earth tones with one coastal lift.

The palette keeps the therapy brand warm and grounded while preserving the original logo's blue-green water note. Colors are named so the suite can become a repeatable system instead of one-off pieces.

Principle 01

Readable first.

Names, credentials, phone numbers, and location should always win. Decorative elements stay behind the message.

Principle 02

Human, not precious.

The suite should feel beautiful, but still useful for referrals, waiting rooms, business cards, and everyday office communication.

Principle 03

Consistent by design.

Repeated color, corner radius, typography, and subtle foliage keep each piece recognizable without making every item identical.

Rounded Business Cards

Three therapist card systems

Each card has a front and back. These are designed as premium rounded-corner cards, not square-edged office cards.

Print finish direction: 32pt soft-touch stock, rounded corners, subtle deboss on the therapist name, and a quiet spot gloss or blind emboss on the lotus/water mark. The mockup uses a fine paper-grain overlay and soft lift so the cards feel tactile without a heavy outline.
