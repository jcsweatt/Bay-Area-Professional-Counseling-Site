About Kayla

Therapy shaped around each individual.

Kayla believes there is no one-size-fits-all approach to counseling. She works individually with clients to understand and tailor evidence-based treatments that fit each person's unique needs and concerns.

Her passion includes providing mental health care services to underserved populations, including individuals in foster care and individuals with dyslexia.

What therapy is like with Kayla

Kayla uses a person-centered approach to establish congruence and genuineness. She aims to make counseling valued, collaborative, and restorative through a safe and non-judgmental environment.
