Insurance & Payment

A note about coverage.

Therapists at Bay Area Professional Counseling LLC are credentialed with various private insurance companies to provide clinical counseling services. Each health insurance policy is different. Please review your policy or check with your insurance carrier to verify whether you have mental health benefits or a deductible that must be met before scheduling an appointment.

If services are not covered, or if a deductible needs to be met, Bay Area Professional Counseling offers self-pay options based on each clinician's rates.

This office is not an emergency service. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. For mental health or substance use crisis support, call or text 988.
