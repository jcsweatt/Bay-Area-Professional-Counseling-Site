About Madison

Compassionate support across ages and needs.

Madison earned her Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from Huntingdon College and completed her Master's in Clinical Mental Health Counseling at the University of West Alabama.

Her professional experience includes work as a Registered Behavior Technician with children of all ages on the autism spectrum, along with internship experience providing individual and group counseling for people navigating substance use disorders.

What therapy is like with Madison

Madison is an Associate Licensed Counselor under the supervision of Lacey Moore, LPC-S. She is passionate about supporting clients of all ages as they work through concerns in a caring and collaborative environment.
