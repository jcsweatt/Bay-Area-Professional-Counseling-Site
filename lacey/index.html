About Lacey

Care focused on strengths, goals, and meaningful change.

Before entering private practice, Lacey acquired nineteen years of clinical experience in addictions and behavioral health across outpatient, community, school, and residential settings. She has been a Licensed Professional Counselor since 2009 and is a Counseling Supervisor with the Alabama Board of Examiners in Counseling.

Lacey earned her Master's Degree in Community Counseling and Bachelor's Degree in Human Services from the University of South Alabama. She is a Master's Addiction Counselor through NAADAC and a member of the Alabama Counseling Association and NAADAC, the Association for Addiction Professionals.

What therapy is like with Lacey

Lacey approaches therapy holistically, helping clients identify needs relating to emotional, psychological, and relational health. She works to provide a non-judgmental relationship that builds coping skills, self-esteem, and the ability to navigate difficult life situations.

Her therapeutic modalities include Solution-Focused Therapy, Empowerment, Motivational Interviewing, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, and Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral techniques.
